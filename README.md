## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🏪 [Toko Karya Abadi](https://github.com/darrenevann/SistemTokoKaryaAbadi)
**Retail Management System** · `Java 21` `Spring Boot` `Spring Security` `JPA` `MySQL` `Chart.js`

A full retail workflow: barcode-driven POS, stock receiving, suppliers, damaged goods, price history, and cash reconciliation. Features role-based access (Admin, Cashier, Owner), BCrypt hashing, CSRF protection, and a sales dashboard with charts and low-stock alerts.

### 🚚 [Expedition Management System](https://github.com/darrenevann/SistemEkspedisiBarang)
**Shipment Management System** · `Java 17` `Spring Boot` `JPA` `MySQL` `Thymeleaf`

Shipment and courier management with automatic fee calculation for Regular, Express, and Cargo services. Applies **inheritance and polymorphism** in a layered MVC architecture. Developed in a team of two.

### 🎓 [Sistem Portal Kampus](https://github.com/darrenevann/SistemPortalKampus)
**Campus Information Portal** · `PHP` `MySQL` `Bootstrap 5`

An academic information portal for thesis registration, supervision, exam scheduling, and scholarship recipient management. Includes a public site and a session-protected admin panel with CRUD across 9 modules on a 13-table database.

### 👛 [Dompet Pintar](https://github.com/darrenevann/DompetPintar)
**Personal Finance App** · `Flutter` `Dart`

A cross-platform finance tracker with a custom neo-brutalist UI, automatic transaction categorization, per-category budgets, a 6-month expense chart, and local data persistence.
